Why an Arcade Room?

$VWFEST is a community of air-cooled VW people first. These games are here for the fun of it: something to play while the grill heats up, something to put on the screen at a meetup, and a friendly leaderboard to talk trash on. The race, zombie and drag leaderboards take a nickname only: no account, no wallet and no prizes, just bragging rights. GUESS THE CHART is the one exception: it has its own free account and a Phantom wallet check for $209madness holders, and its own rules.
